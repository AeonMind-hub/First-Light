    Q.01How long does a typical project take?

    Landing sites run 2–3 weeks, full websites 4–6 weeks, and mobile or web apps 8–12 weeks to a first release. Your proposal includes a week-by-week timeline — and we hit it.

    Q.02How does pricing work?

    Fixed price, agreed in writing before kickoff: 40% to start, 40% at the midpoint demo, 20% at launch. Programmes above $40k are phased by milestone so risk stays low on both sides.

    Q.03Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

    Yes — most of our work is remote across Africa, Europe and North America. We guarantee at least four hours of timezone overlap, weekly video demos, and a shared board you can check any day.

    Q.04Who owns the work when it’s done?

    You do — completely. Code lives in your repository from day one, designs in your Figma, and full IP transfer is written into every contract upon final payment.

    Q.05Can you take over an existing product?

    Often, yes. We start with a paid technical and design audit — a fixed-fee document covering what to keep, what to fix and what to rebuild — then you decide whether we’re the ones to do it.

    Q.06What happens after launch?

    Every launch includes 30 days of fixes at no cost. After that, most clients move to a retainer for iteration and experiments — but there’s no obligation, and we hand over clean documentation either way.

    Q.07Can you add AI to a product we already have?

    Yes — most AI work starts exactly there. We begin with a fixed-fee pilot behind a feature flag: one measurable use-case (support drafting, document Q&A, workflow automation), benchmarked for two weeks. If the numbers hold, we roll it out properly. If they don’t, we say so and you keep the pilot code.
