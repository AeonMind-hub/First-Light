Process

The path to launch

  1. Discover WEEK 1

    We interrogate the goal, not just the brief. You leave with a scoped proposal, fixed price and week-by-week timeline.

  2. Design WEEKS 1–3

    Wireframes to high-fidelity UI on real content. You click through a prototype before a line of code is written.

  3. Build WEEKS 2–8

    Clean documented code on a live staging link. Friday demos keep scope honest.

  4. Launch LAUNCH WEEK

    Performance, accessibility, technical SEO and a choreographed deploy. We stay on the dashboards for 72 hours.

  5. Support & scale ONGOING

    30 days of included fixes, then retainers for iteration and growth.
